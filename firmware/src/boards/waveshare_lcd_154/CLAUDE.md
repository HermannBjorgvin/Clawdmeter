# LCD-1.54 — `waveshare_lcd_154`

Hardware notes for this board. Shared firmware rules are in `firmware/CLAUDE.md`.

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.54 (ST7789, 240×240 square, CST816T touch @ 0x15). Build env: `waveshare_lcd_154`. **The first non-AMOLED port**: a plain 4-wire SPI TFT, not QSPI, and the panel has no brightness command — backlight is LEDC PWM on `LCD_BL`. **No PMU**: battery is an ADC divider on GPIO1 and `BAT_EN` (GPIO2) is a power-hold line that must be driven HIGH early in `board_init()` or the board browns out on battery. Three buttons (BOOT + GPIO5 + a PWR-role GPIO4); ES8311 chime wired up; QMI8658 populated but unused (fixed orientation, no rotation).
